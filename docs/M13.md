# M13 — continuous player movement, collision and camera

## Resume here

**M13 extends the M8–M12 reconstruction to continuous, input-driven player movement:**
each frame begins from the preceding reconstructed frame's carried state instead of a
captured original dispatcher-entry state. One authenticated original-game starting
state, the recorded controller input, and the reconstructed per-frame path produce 20+
consecutive frames covering free approach, wall contact, sliding turn in contact,
release, braking to zero and stationary updates, with history and the same-frame camera
on every frame.

The inter-frame gap is closed by newly reconstructed original behavior: the observed
prefix of the per-frame coordinator `0012f850` (the `3fa190` frame-counter increment
with its negative-wrap reset (`bgez` guard), the `0031bb80` edge-counter sync, the `3f9ff8` clear and
the guarded `46e118` increment) and the engine counter advance body `001379f0` of
`00137890` (`46d930`/`46e110` increments with the −1 clamp and the 108000-frame
mega-counter path). The coordinator's remaining engine service calls are declared
external host seams; their memory effects are provably outside every reconstructed
domain in the observed frames.

## Dispatch contract

The continuous frame's bus dispatcher (`frame_process_call`) distinguishes two call
classes, verified against the authenticated coordinator disassembly
(`local/m13/coordinator-12f850.dump.txt`, ELF `a74248c5…3c61`):

- **Declared external** (host-only `HG_EXTERNAL_CALL` seam): the coordinator
  `0012f850` engine-service jal targets `00198f60`, `0017f6d0`, `0017f2d0`,
  `0013b6e0`, `001430a0`, `00143100`, `0012bc70`, `0018c520`, `00143150`,
  `001431a0`, `0025c170`, `00182480`, `0013ce80` (authenticated at
  `0012f8a8…0012f92c`). Their memory effects land in the inter-frame external
  domains (0 player/camera/counter bytes in all 11 diffed pairs). The final
  coordinator call `00189be0` (`0012f934`) is likewise outside every reconstructed
  domain and is not emitted.
- **Original-instruction interpreter** (owner bus, `FP_SEAMS` operand placement):
  the camera-chain matrix helpers `00143c60`/`00143d30`/`00143b90` reached from
  `0012bd00`/`0012c790`. They appear nowhere in the coordinator; an earlier
  revision externalized them, leaving the head matrix identity, so the camera
  struct diverged from the first continuous frame. `test_player_continuous.py::
  test_frame_dispatch_routes_camera_helpers_to_interpreter` locks the routing.

### Render-facing scratch exclusion (data-driven)

The verifier computes the excluded scratch offsets from the authenticated
observation captures themselves, never from a hardcoded list: the union over
consecutive frame pairs of offsets the ORIGINAL changes (a) between frame N's
camera-return and frame N+1's coordinator-entry (inter-frame render work) and
(b) between coordinator-entry and dispatch-entry (the declared-external
services `00198f60`/`0017f6d0`/`0017f2d0`). For obs3 that is 169 offsets,
e.g. `0x700=[1718,1926,2378,2170]` and `0xba0=[64,64,64,127]` at every
dispatch-entry..camera-entry stop, the `0x500` head matrix rewritten
inter-frame, and the `0x7b0` screen-space pair `0,0 -> 41.916,13.167`.

At the camera-return stage the offsets the reconstructed path actually writes
are guarded and must reproduce the original's post-chain values (they do:
0 guarded differences). The offsets the path never writes stay external at
every stage — screen-space projection arrays `0x7c0–0x7ff` (quad corners
−6.7/65.3/28.94541 with camera-tracking depth and +72 offsets), `0x900`
(normalized screen coordinate, linear in the eye position), `0x940` (constant
165.8/180/495/1), and the constant render block `0x710..0x72f`
(0,768,960,0,128,128,128,12) with `0xba0..0xbaf` (camera position, w=127.0).
The authenticated `0012c790` stream contains no stores to them and every
direct writer is `jalr`-indirect — deeper chain paths the composition does not
model. At every other stage the carried C value is a previous-frame camera
output while the original's capture holds its own external rewrite, so the
whole set is classified external and enumerated. The reconstructed camera
state `0x3f9860` and all RAM guarded domains are compared at every stage.

The camera-chain matrix helpers `00143c60`/`00143d30`/`00143b90` execute original
instructions through the owner bus in both the M12 and the continuous paths
(`test_player_continuous.py` locks the routing); the continuous verifier's
camera-return stage is snapshotted at the `0013b6e0` seam (`0012f8d8`, the first
tail service after the chain — the original's stop is `0012f8d4`), so the
`001379f0` counter advance that follows it is never compared against the
pre-advance capture.

A tempting but incorrect fix was made and reverted on the M12 gate's
evidence: an unconditional reconstruction of the camera-return render stores
(`0xba0.w = 127.0` and the constant block `0x710..0x72f`) failed the M12
turn-camera instruction differential, whose EE reference executes the original
`0012c790` stream — the original's own stream contains no stores to either
region, and every direct writer is `jalr`-indirect (deeper chain paths the
composition does not model). Both regions are therefore declared render-facing
external at every stage; the camera state and every offset the path writes
remain guarded.

## Limits

- The continuous sequence is verified for the obs3 corridor profile only: 22
  frames (10164–10185), 6 approach, 2 contact, 4 contact-turn (12 push stops),
  6 release, 4 stopped. No other route, save state or input sequence is claimed.
- The coordinator's engine-service calls (`00198f60`, `0017f6d0`, `0017f2d0`,
  tail services, `00189be0`) remain declared external host seams; their memory
  effects land in the enumerated external domains (0 player/camera/counter
  bytes in the inter-frame evidence).
- The original `0012c790`-body screen-space projection arrays (`0x7c0–0x7ff`,
  `0x900`, `0x940`) are render-facing external at every stage: the C
  composition does not model the body's projection math (view-matrix rows,
  VU-level output). The camera state `0x3f9860` and every scratch offset the
  path writes are fully guarded.
- The dispatcher remains the observed state-1 active path; other player states
  fail closed (0x30). The floor response keeps its M12 fail-closed paths
  (airborne, hazard, special-surface, type-5, spawn).
- Between frames only the recorded pad-area input is supplied externally;
  every other byte is carried from the preceding reconstructed frame.
